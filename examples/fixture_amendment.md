Amendment to the SaaS Schedule to the Master Relationship Agreement ("Amendment")
between Example Analytics, Inc. ("Provider") and Northwind Retail L.P. ("Customer")
effective July 7, 2022 ("SaaS Schedule")

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000003'></a>

WHEREAS, Provider and Customer are parties to the SaaS Schedule with an effective date of
July 7, 2022;

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000004'></a>

WHEREAS, the parties desire to amend the SaaS Schedule;

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000005'></a>

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises and covenants contained in this Amendment and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto, intending to be legally bound, hereby agree as follows:

* The parties agree that the "Additional Acceptance Criteria" section of the SaaS Schedule shall be deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000006'></a>

First party Integration with Customer:
* Direct, file transfer integration with the customer ingest platform into Provider where Provider will encrypt, store and process personal data for insights and audience activation.
* Provider will commit to scoping for hybrid compute in Phase I and implementation in Q2 calendar 2023 pending Customer IT and security dependencies.
* Provider is SSO compliant with Customer approved standards at the end of Phase I go live.
* Data aggregation, normalization, and cleansing
* Website tag implementation - Customer has the flexibility to place the Provider tag in the header or the footer

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000007'></a>

Business User Flexibility, Audience Segmentation and Activation:
* Provider to build out a second production instance specifically to separate personal data from third party data
* Business users can leverage suppression capabilities to suppress targeted profiles from paid campaigns
* Business users will have the ability to build basic and composite, custom-built audiences
* Business users can analyze audience overlaps and iterate on audience definitions

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000008'></a>

Build and Activate Audiences:
* Build audiences with a combination of first party and third party data
* Reach prospects and customers through demand side platform activation with acquisition and retargeting use cases
* Activate through the available advertising platforms

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-000000000009'></a>

Acceptance Period Timing:

<a id='b1000000-0000-4000-8000-00000000000a'></a>

The Acceptance Period shall begin on the Effective Date and shall end thirty (30) days
following the date on which Provider notifies Customer that the Phase I deliverables are
available for review. If Customer does not deliver a written rejection notice within the
Acceptance Period, the deliverables shall be deemed accepted.
